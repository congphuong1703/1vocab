Privacy policy

Updated: October 26, 2026 · Tiếng Việt

You do not need a separate 1vocab account. Learning progress is stored on your device. The app uses these services to provide its features.

1vocab does not include the words you study, your name, email address or feedback text in analytics events. Technical identifiers can identify an app installation; they are not a named 1vocab account.

Ads and your choices

The app presents consent choices where required. On iOS, tracking across other companies’ apps or websites is requested only with your App Tracking Transparency permission. You can keep learning if you decline. Contextual ads may still appear.

Where applicable, reopen ad privacy options in app Settings. System tracking permission is controlled in device settings. See Google’s policy and RevenueCat’s policy.

Payments and entitlements

The App Store or Google Play handles payment details; 1vocab does not collect or store bank card numbers. RevenueCat receives transaction and entitlement information to verify purchases, renewals and restores, including subscriptions purchased before new features launch. The app caches entitlements and the last verification time locally to support offline access. See the purchase and subscription terms for duration, renewal and refund details.

Optional sync and retention

iOS uses a private iCloud zone. Android requests only the Google Drive appDataFolder scope and does not read other Drive documents. The developer has no administrative interface to read progress in your cloud account. Apple and Google process cloud data under their policies. Sync does not work across iOS and Android.

Local data remains until you clear it or uninstall. Cloud data remains until you request deletion. SDK retention depends on each service and its applicable configuration; contact us with data requests.

Delete data or disable features

  • Clear learning progress: Settings > Delete learning data. With sync enabled, this reset reaches other devices on their next sync.
  • Delete the cloud copy while keeping local progress: Settings > Delete synced data, then confirm. Sync turns off after successful deletion.
  • Disable notifications: in the app or system notification settings.

Deleting progress does not cancel store purchases or subscriptions. Manage subscriptions through Apple or Google.

Feedback, children and contact

Feedback email is sent only when you send it from your mail app. The developer receives your sending address, message and technical details you choose to include, for support. Do not send passwords or payment information.

The app is not designed or marketed specifically to children. Contact us if you believe a child’s data has been provided inappropriately.
